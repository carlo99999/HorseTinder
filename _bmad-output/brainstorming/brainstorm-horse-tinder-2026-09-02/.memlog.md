- (direction by user) This is a joke project for trying BMad; the app is for horses, not horse owners.
- (technique) started Three Rounds of Stupid
- (idea by coach) Horses swipe by booping a giant carrot-shaped screen with their nose; a mutual match triggers a burst of oats.
- (direction by user) Horses use their fingers, in a BoJack Horseman-style world.
- (idea by user) The horses have no patience, so Horse Tinder must be extremely fast.
- (idea by coach) Profiles are only visible for three galloping seconds; hesitate and the app declares the horse too emotionally unavailable and moves on.
- (idea by user) A match opens a chat so the horses can fall in love.
- (idea by coach) The chat has an impatient-heart mode: messages appear as one-tap reactions such as 'neigh', 'hay?', and a tiny galloping-horse GIF, with an optional real text field for unexpectedly earnest romance.
- (idea by user) A horse is an instant deal-breaker after being marked emotionally unavailable more than three times.
- (idea by coach) After the third emotionally-unavailable mark, a horse profile gets a dramatic red-flag badge; only a grand romantic gesture in chat can remove it.
- (idea by coach) A grand gesture is hiring a tiny skywriter to spell 'I'M READY TO BE VULNERABLE' above the stable, then immediately deleting the message from embarrassment.
- (direction by user) Grand romantic gestures should be completely unhinged.
- (idea by coach) A horse recruits an entire marching band of miniature ponies to spell its crush's name in hoofprints across every field in town, then claims it was an accident.
- (idea by user) The recipient replies to a grand gesture with an even bigger gesture.
- (insight by coach) Horse Tinder romance is a competitive escalation: each gesture invites an even bigger one.
- (technique) started Cursed Genie
- (idea by user) Matching every horse in the world at once means messages never deliver.
- (idea by coach) The unread-message counter becomes an infinity symbol, and every attempted 'neigh' is swallowed by a spinning hay bale loading icon.
- (idea by user) To fix undelivered messages, an impatient horse must either pay or wait 30 days.
- (idea by coach) The premium upgrade is 'Stable Plus': it promises delivery now, but its checkout countdown lasts 30 days unless the horse pays again.
- (idea by user) The most shameless premium feature is a real genie that genuinely grants wishes.
- (insight) The core product joke is an ultra-fast, BoJack-style horse dating app where impatience and predatory premium mechanics become romantic obstacles. Key loop: rapid swiping, mutual match opens chat, emotionally-unavailable strikes create red flags, and grand gestures escalate competitively. Stable Plus and its real genie make the monetization gag the climax.
